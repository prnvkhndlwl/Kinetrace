# Code of conduct

Kinetrace is made for researchers, students and anyone who studies how animals
and people move. We want everyone who reports a problem, asks a question or
contributes to feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or field.

This code follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to tracking, to
  programming or to GitHub. There are no stupid questions here.
- Assume good intent; criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; say thank you.
- Respect people's data: never post someone else's footage, recordings or
  personal details without their permission.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone, for any reason.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional or classroom
  setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki) and wherever
someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately
through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile
([@prnvkhndlwl](https://github.com/prnvkhndlwl)). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues or pull
requests that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.
